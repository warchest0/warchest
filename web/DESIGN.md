# Warchest website — seal edition, 2026-10-12

The site follows the brand chosen for launch: the wax seal (`brand/logos/seal/`).

- **Palette:** bone paper `#ece4d2` with a light grain, ink `#0f0e0c`, wax red `#a3221b` (brighter `#e0503c` on dark grounds). One dark section (ranks) and one wax section (Siege) break the page rhythm.
- **Type:** Anton for display headings, JetBrains Mono for body and figures, UnifrakturCook only inside the wordmark and seal. All self-hosted.
- **Hero:** the seal is pressed onto the page on load (scale, rotate, blur out), next to "Hold longer. Rank higher."
- **Motion:** seal press, scroll reveals, a ribbon of the seal's words, a price line drawn in the Siege chart. Everything stops under `prefers-reduced-motion` or the footer switch.
- **Product truth:** pre-launch, not deployed, audit pending. The Siege chart is labelled as an illustration. The status list says what is built and what is in progress; no fictional TVL, yields or holder data. The earlier voting and off-chain trading design is described as retired, not hidden.

The previous edition (blue globe, governance copy) is in the git history before this change.
