# Warchest website — night edition with the seal, 2026-10-12

A blend of the first edition (dark ground, glass header, WebGL globe, light middle section) and the wax seal chosen for launch (`brand/logos/seal/`), with terminal details from the v3 brand study.

- **Palette:** warm night `#0b0a09`, bone `#ece4d2`, wax red `#a3221b` and `#e0503c`. The hero globe is tinted sepia with a wax rim; the treasury section is bone paper with a wax-red dotted globe; the closing band is wax red with the seal embossed.
- **Type:** Inter for headings and body, JetBrains Mono for labels, figures and terminals. Gothic lettering only inside the seal and wordmark SVGs. All self-hosted.
- **Terminal details:** a shell prompt above the hero title, the rank calculator and the status list drawn as terminal windows, `// 01` section labels.
- **Motion:** globe rotation (pause and zoom controls), scroll reveals, a blinking cursor. Everything stops under `prefers-reduced-motion` or the footer switch; the globes fall back to a 2D canvas or a static image without WebGL.
- **Product truth:** pre-launch, not deployed, audit pending. The treasury globe is labelled as a conceptual view. No fictional TVL, yields or holder data.

The seal-only edition and the first edition are in the git history.
