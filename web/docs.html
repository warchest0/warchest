01 / The fee

$WAR is a fixed-supply ERC-20 with no transfer tax in the token itself. A Uniswap v4 hook takes 10% of every buy and every sell through the official pool and sends it to the chest, always in ETH. Ordinary wallet transfers and liquidity operations pay nothing.

02 / Ranks

Every batch of tokens you receive is a lot. A lot starts at rank 0 on the day it arrives and climbs one rank per full UTC day, up to rank 10. The indexer computes this from the token's transfer history.

Ranks, from 0 to 10: Recruit, Soldier, Corporal, Sergeant, Lieutenant, Captain, Commander, Colonel, General, Marshal, Warlord.

Selling part of a bag

A sale or transfer uses your newest lots first. Your older tokens keep the rank they earned; only the sold part is gone. A wallet that receives tokens starts a new lot at rank 0. There is no way to buy a rank.

03 / Rewards

What the chest distributes is split between holders by weight:

weight = Σ (lot amount × rank)

The same bag at rank 10 weighs five times the same bag at rank 2. A lot on its first day has no weight yet. The weight decides a share of what is distributed; it is not a promised return.

04 / Siege mode

When the price is more than 30% under its all-time high, Siege arms. While it lasts, the chest buys back half of every sell, with no daily cap, and every token it buys back is burned.

Siege uses the chest's liquid reserve; it is a defence of the price floor, not a guarantee against losses.

05 / The treasury

The chest keeps 20% liquid for buybacks. When that reserve falls to 5%, it refills from the rest of the treasury. The remaining funds go to audited on-chain yield vaults; the provider is still being chosen. That yield is what holders share by rank.

Conversions from ETH use on-chain time-weighted prices with slippage bounds and a circuit breaker that refuses to convert while the short-term price strays too far from its 6-hour average.

06 / Status
