The market
fills the chest.

Three rules. Activity builds the treasury, time decides who it rewards, and nobody can buy their way to the top.

10%of every trade

Every trade fills the chest.

Every buy and every sell sends 10% to one shared treasury, collected in ETH by a Uniswap v4 hook. Ordinary wallet transfers pay nothing.

10ranks, one per day

Every day you hold, you rank up.

Your tokens climb one rank per full day held, from Recruit to Warlord. Sell part of your bag and only the sold part loses its rank: your oldest tokens keep theirs.

×Rtokens × rank

Higher rank, bigger share.

What the chest earns is shared by tokens × rank. Same bag at rank 10 weighs five times a bag at rank 2. Time is the only way up.

    The chest
    fights back.

    Siege mode. When the price falls more than 30% under its all-time high, the chest starts buying, and everything it buys is burned.

    1. −30%Price falls more than 30% under its all-time high: Siege arms.
    2. 50%The chest buys back half of every sell while Siege lasts. No daily cap.
    3. 100%Every token it buys back is burned. Supply only goes down.

    A chest that
    never sleeps.

    Idle funds earn yield in on-chain vaults, a liquid reserve stays ready for Siege, and holders share the yield by rank.

    20%

    Liquid reserve

    A fifth of the chest stays liquid for buybacks. When it falls to 5%, it refills itself from the rest of the treasury.

    80%

    Put to work

    The rest is deposited in audited on-chain yield vaults. The provider is still being chosen.

    Shared by rank

    The yield is what holders share, weighted by tokens × rank. No rank is for sale; it can only be earned with time.
